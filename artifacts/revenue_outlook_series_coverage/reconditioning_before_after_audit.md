## A note on the snapshot format

The pinned snapshot is parquet, not CSV. `pandas.to_csv` does not round-trip
float64 exactly in this environment: a plain round-trip moved 476 of 8,344
`annual_source_value` cells by up to 7.3e-12, and `float_format='%.17g'` moved
1,458. A CSV snapshot would therefore have made "annual benchmarks unchanged,
max delta exactly 0" impossible to state - the storage would have injected
noise of the same order as the thing being measured. Parquet stores float64
bit-exactly, so the zero above is a real zero.

## What this audit does not prove

Cross-platform stability is asserted by CI, not here: this script runs
on one machine and can only compare two committed packs. The
cross-platform arm is `test_committed_pack_matches_a_fresh_build`
running on the Linux runner against the Windows-built committed pack,
under the declared comparison (structurally exact, values within 1e-9
relative). That tolerance was NOT widened to accommodate the badly
scaled solve - it stayed at 1e-9 and the solve was fixed instead.
