### A seventh correction I found while implementing

Applying isotonic smoothing directly to `q10`/`q90` conflates two different
things: the **location** (a median bias that drifts with horizon) and the
**dispersion**. These forecasts carry a real downward bias — the median
actual/forecast ratio at the seam is 0.9395 (Light RUC), 0.9482 (PED), 0.9738
(Heavy RUC) — so forcing the raw quantiles to be monotone dragged that drifting
bias into the width. The basis now decomposes into median plus half-widths,
smooths **only the half-widths**, and carries the median through untouched.
`test_dispersion_is_monotone_but_bias_is_not_forced` pins it.

## 2. The generated June-year basis

Cohort `all_available`, all targets. `june_year_uncertainty_basis.csv`.

**80% span (%), raw → smoothed**

| stream | JY H1 (FY2026) | H2 | H3 | H4 | **H5 (FY2030 seam)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PED | 3.17 | 9.57 | 11.69 | 11.65 | **16.84** (raw 12.68) |
| HEAVY_RUC | 5.47 | 8.10 | 9.64 | 9.53 | **10.41** (raw 9.72) |
| LIGHT_RUC | 16.12 | 21.00 | 20.94 | 26.92 | **32.19** (raw 23.11) |

**The smoothed seam is wider than the raw seam for every stream**, and the owner
should see why before this ships. The monotone-dispersion constraint lifts thin
late cells to the level of earlier, better-sampled ones:

- **PED**: the raw H5 lower half-width *drops* to 0.0540 from 0.1149/0.1115 at
  H3/H4 while the median simultaneously falls to −0.0531. That cell is
  "narrower but shifted". Smoothing keeps the shift and restores the earlier
  width → 16.84%.
- **Light RUC**: the raw H5 upper half-width collapses to 0.0323 from 0.1754 at
  H2; smoothing holds it at 0.1217. The lower half-width genuinely grows to
  0.2398.

This is the specified method behaving as specified — "uncertainty must not
shrink with horizon" — but it is a methodological choice worth an explicit yes,
because on the raw values the seam spans would be 12.68 / 9.72 / 23.11.

### Asymmetry at the seam

| stream | lower distance | upper distance | ratio | median multiplier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PED | **14.32%** | 2.52% | 0.18 | 0.9482 |
| LIGHT_RUC | **26.08%** | 6.11% | 0.23 | 0.9395 |
| HEAVY_RUC | **6.28%** | 4.13% | 0.66 | 0.9738 |

The bands are strongly one-sided: realised outcomes have come in *below*
forecast far more often than above. A symmetric band would have placed PED's
lower edge at 8.4% instead of 14.3% and hidden the bias entirely.

### Origin-clustered bootstrap on the seam

| stream | origins | q10 | q10 p05–p95 | q90 | q90 p05–p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PED | 18 | −0.1071 | −0.1221 … −0.0860 | 0.0249 | 0.0140 … 0.0284 |
| HEAVY_RUC | 12 | −0.0575 | −0.0649 … −0.0340 | 0.0405 | −0.0234 … 0.0508 |
| LIGHT_RUC | **6** | −0.3022 | **−0.3131 … −0.0664** | −0.0301 | −0.0624 … −0.0229 |

**Light RUC's seam rests on six origins**, and its q10 bootstrap spans a factor
of 4.7. The width is retained per the owner decision — not capped, not narrowed
— but the audit carries this so nobody reads 32% as a precise number. Heavy
RUC's q90 bootstrap crosses zero, which is the same caution in milder form.

## 3. Indicative aggregate bracket (corrected)

Leaves sharing one parent shock are summed first — conventional Light, Light
BEV and PHEV all ride a single Light factor and are *not* independent — and only
the three independent parents (PED, LIGHT_RUC, HEAVY_RUC) are then bracketed.

Total NLTF 80% span (%):

| basis | FY2027 | FY2030 | FY2031 | FY2040 | FY2050 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| independent across parents | 6.86 | 11.50 | 11.62 | 14.48 | 20.84 |
| comonotonic across parents | 12.29 | 19.17 | 19.27 | 21.39 | 24.64 |

Indicative only. The production aggregate band must come from draw-level
`FORMULA_DEFINITIONS` propagation with estimated cross-stream dependence. The
span still grows after FY2031 under a flat per-stream plateau because the
*composition* shifts — Light classes grow as a share of the total.

## 4. Series-tier contract and the four proxies

`uncertainty_series_contract_draft.csv` — 3 direct, 13 derived, 4 proxy.

| leaf | resolution | calibration | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `heavy_bev_ruc_net_km` / `_revenue` | Heavy RUC relative error factor, applied **identically to km and revenue** so the effective-rate identity closes | rolling origins 2012Q1–2025Q4, n=12 origins at the seam | pending |
| `net_mvr_revenue` | BEFU26-vs-MBU26 vintage revision. The vintages agree exactly in 24 of 25 forecast years; the single differing year is 7.64%. p10/p90 of that revision is **0**, which would give a zero band, so the **full observed revision range** is adopted as the 80% span (±7.35/7.94%) and half as the 50% — deliberately wider than the measured quantiles | FY2026–FY2050 committed vintages | pending |
| `tuc_net_revenue` | **Zero modelled uncertainty.** TUC is 17.56 in every FY and every vintage: a governed fixed administrative component, not a model output. A band around it would assert something no evidence supports | — | pending |

No single arbitrary percentage is used for MVR and TUC; they get different,
separately justified treatments. The limitation that **the Total NLTF band does
not cover TUC** is carried in the contract.

Light BEV / PHEV are **not** proxies: one Light model-error factor scales the
governed pool, which is then allocated through the selected exact VFM shares,
preserving perfect dependence among the three class kilometres conditional on
those shares. No conventional draw is divided by a shrinking share, and lambda
stays retired.

## 5. Conditional, throughout

The rolling-origin evaluation is **actual-driver**: exogenous inputs at each
target are the observed values. It isolates model degradation with horizon and
**understates** true forward error, which also carries Treasury-driver
forecast error. Every state name carries `conditional` for that reason, and no
label may claim the band includes driver uncertainty.

The one committed source that *does* include driver error — `mot_error_bands.csv`
(archived MoT forecast error, n up to 52, June-year horizons) — covers 7 series
but not MVR or TUC. It is the level cross-check, not a substitute.
