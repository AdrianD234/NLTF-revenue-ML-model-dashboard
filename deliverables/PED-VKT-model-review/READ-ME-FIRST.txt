===========================================================================
PETROL VEHICLE TRAVEL MODEL - SPECIFICATION AND DATA
===========================================================================

PURPOSE

Quarterly forecast of light petrol vehicle kilometres travelled per capita
in New Zealand.

This quantity subsequently feeds a fuel excise revenue forecast through a
separate chain covering population, fleet composition and fuel efficiency.
That chain is out of scope here. This pack covers the travel equation only.


CONTENTS

  READ-ME-FIRST.txt          specification, data notes, accuracy record
  ped-vkt-data.dta           estimation data, Stata format
  ped-vkt-data.csv           the same data, comma separated
  run-in-stata.do            optional starter script
  ped-vkt-forecast-test.csv  out of sample forecast record


===========================================================================
SPECIFICATION
===========================================================================

Dependent variable is log kilometres per capita per quarter.

  log(VKT per capita)
      =  constant
       + b1  x log(real petrol price)
       + b2  x log(real GDP per capita)
       + b3  x unemployment rate
       + b4  x trend
       + b5  x (trend, 2011 onward)
       + b6  x (indicator, 2020 onward)
       + b7  x (indicator, calendar 2020)
       + b8  x Q2  +  b9 x Q3  +  b10 x Q4
       + b11 x log(VKT per capita, lagged one quarter)
       + u

  u(t) = rho x u(t-1) + e(t)

Estimator is feasible GLS with AR(1) errors, fitted in logs on an expanding
window. Maximum eight iterations. Q1 is the omitted season.


COEFFICIENTS

  constant                                   1.9383
  log real petrol price                     -0.0628
  log real GDP per capita                    0.1966
  unemployment rate                          0.1356
  trend                                     -0.0012
  trend from 2011                           -0.0001
  2020 onward                               -0.0237
  calendar 2020                             -0.0036
  Q2                                         0.0014
  Q3                                         0.0091
  Q4                                         0.0194
  lagged dependent variable                  0.5342

  rho                                        0.5233

The first two are short run elasticities. With a lagged dependent variable
of 0.5342 the implied long run multiplier is about 2.15, giving a long run
petrol price elasticity near -0.135.

Standard errors are not carried in the production state. Any inference you
need will have to be re-estimated.


===========================================================================
DATA
===========================================================================

96 quarters, 2002 Q1 to 2025 Q4. The estimation sample is 95 quarters,
2002 Q2 to 2025 Q4. Column "insample" flags those 95.

2002 Q1 is retained solely to supply the first lag. It is a complete
observation but cannot itself be estimated on.



VARIABLES

  period          quarter label, e.g. 2002Q1
  qdate           quarter as a Stata date. In the .dta this is a proper
                  quarterly variable and displays as 2002q1. In the .csv it
                  is the underlying integer on Stata's scale, where
                  1960 Q1 = 0, so 2002 Q1 = 168 and 2025 Q4 = 263. Use
                  "period" if you would rather read the label.
  vktpc           kilometres per capita per quarter - dependent variable
  lnvktpc         log of vktpc
  lnvktpclag1     lnvktpc lagged one quarter
  petrolprice     real petrol price, cents per litre
  lnpetrol        log of petrolprice
  gdppc           real GDP per capita, NZD
  lngdppc         log of gdppc
  unemppct        unemployment in percentage points, e.g. 5.3
  unemprate       unemployment as a decimal, e.g. 0.053 - USED IN MODEL
  population      supplied for context, not in the equation
  trend           quarters since 2000 Q1, where 2000 Q1 = 1
  post2011        indicator, year >= 2011
  post2011trend   trend x post2011
  post2020        indicator, year >= 2020
  covid2020       indicator, calendar 2020 only
  q2, q3, q4      seasonal indicators, Q1 omitted
  insample        1 for the 95 estimation quarters


THREE CONVENTIONS WORTH CONFIRMING BEFORE YOU ESTIMATE

  1. Unemployment enters as a decimal fraction, not percentage points.
     The coefficient of 0.1356 therefore corresponds to roughly 0.14 per
     cent on VKT per capita for a one percentage point move in the
     unemployment rate. The column unemppct holds the percentage point
     series if you prefer that scale.

  2. Unemployment is the only driver not in logs.

  3. The trend is an uncentred quarter counter from 2000 Q1, and
     post2011trend is its product with the post 2011 indicator rather than
     a separate slope dummy.


===========================================================================
OUT OF SAMPLE PERFORMANCE
===========================================================================

Rolling origin, actual drivers. Mean absolute percentage error:

  1 quarter ahead        1.1
  4 quarters ahead       2.0
  12 quarters ahead      4.3
  20 quarters ahead      6.0

Two qualifications.

Horizons out to 12 quarters sit inside the range covered by the governed
backtest evidence. Horizons 13 to 20 have been tested but on thinner
samples. Beyond 20 quarters there is no comparable evidence.

Mean signed error moves from -0.1 per cent at one quarter to +4.3 per cent
at twenty. The model over predicts at longer horizons rather than erring
symmetrically. Excluding 2020 and 2021 targets reduces this but does not
remove it.

Quarter by quarter detail is in ped-vkt-forecast-test.csv.


===========================================================================
OPEN QUESTIONS
===========================================================================

Set out for your assessment rather than as settled positions.

  Unemployment carries a positive coefficient. The expected sign is
  negative. It may be proxying for something correlated with the post 2011
  and post 2020 controls.

  The lagged dependent variable and the AR(1) error term are close in
  magnitude, 0.5342 and 0.5233. Whether both are separately identified is a
  reasonable question.

  The deterministic trend, the 2011 slope change and the 2020 level shift
  carry substantial explanatory weight between them. The break dates were
  imposed rather than estimated.

  The short run petrol price elasticity of -0.063 is low relative to the
  published road fuel demand literature, and the implied long run value of
  about -0.135 remains low.

  Levels are recovered by exponentiating with no smearing adjustment, so
  forecasts are conditional medians rather than conditional means.


===========================================================================
SCOPE OF THE ASSURANCE
===========================================================================

The model and data are version controlled and reproduce deterministically,
so this pack and the production system are demonstrably the same object.

That is a statement about reproducibility, not about specification. The
economic question is the one being put to you.
